**Vision-based weight capture.** Aperture measures the weight of an item from a photograph. Built for operations where conventional scales create a bottleneck at receiving, pack out, or fulfillment, and for workflows where items vary enough in shape or volume that a single scale station cannot keep pace.

## Accuracy
- **Production:** 92–95%, verified against scale-calibrated ground truth
- **Ceiling:** up to 98% under tested conditions
- **Quality:** readings individually auditable; every capture logged

## Deployment
- **On-prem (default):** runs on customer hardware; images and readings stay on site
- **Cloud:** available for sites preferring hosted setup
- **Network:** no outbound connectivity required; suitable for restricted networks

## Integration
- Reads into existing inventory, WMS, and ERP systems
- Runs in the browser — no client install on capture devices
- Onboarding, tuning, and ongoing support included in every engagement

## Where it fits
Receiving and shipping · parcel/logistics dim-weight verification · mail sort and fulfillment reconciliation · packaging and QC · inventory reconciliation against manifests · regulated intake (donation/grant/compliance reporting) · retail back of house · agriculture and food handling (pack out, subscription fulfillment, post-harvest grading).

## Availability
In commercial use with limited item-type scope while multi-sector support is finalized. Accepting select partners. Pricing per engagement: site count, camera count, existing camera infrastructure, integration scope. Live demo on request; deeper technical material under NDA.
